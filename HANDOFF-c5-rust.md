## Workflow (important, learned the hard way)
* Heavy runs: ONLY remote-host via ~/.local/share/offload/h1run (gate: MemAvailable >= 11 GB); queue lines in ~/demo-state/H1-QUEUE.md; `tests/h1_retry.sh NAME ENV -- CMD...` retries every 5 min while busy (ENV rust|rvt).
